Understanding your audit

Completed: the requirement is satisfied, including recorded approved waivers.

In progress: coursework is underway. Successful completion is still required.

Needs attention: additional coursework or another requirement remains.

Needs advisor review: the available record cannot establish an outcome. It is not a finding that you failed a requirement.

Getting started

  1. Open PAWS → My degree audit to review your requirements.
  2. Open Remaining requirements for explanations and possible course options.
  3. Use myCAPs to save planned courses by semester. Planning a course does not enroll you.
  4. Schedule Builder opens myCoyote. Sign in, then choose Academics → Schedule Planner.
  5. Choose your catalog year when using the Course Catalog and UGRD Roadmaps.
